Stroke Risk Prediction and Care Planning

This workflow supports stroke risk assessment from patient intake details and provides source-supported educational care planning guidance for clinician review.

Open Patient Intake

What this app does

  • Provides risk assessment output to support clinician decision-making.
  • Generates source-supported guidance from curated healthcare documents.
  • Creates a patient-friendly care plan and a 7-day care calendar.

Important note

This tool is for educational support only. It does not diagnose stroke and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
